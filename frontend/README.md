# Frontend
React/Vite client for the AI Interview & Assessment System.

The assessment contains 30 items: 10 technical MCQs, 6 behavioral, 2 coding, 5 communication and 7 situation-based. Communication is interactive and includes reading, storytelling, explanation, customer role-play and opinion speaking.

The browser blocks copy/paste/context menu during the interview, logs tab switches, monitors camera presence and multiple faces, and keeps the webcam visible throughout the assessment. These are integrity signals, not proof of misconduct. Browser security cannot guarantee that a candidate cannot use another device.
